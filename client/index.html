Field manual

Click or tap terrain to walk toward it. Drag the world to orbit the camera. Drag the movement pad for precise movement; use the action buttons to gather, build, rotate and place. Hold Gather to keep collecting. Wheel or +/− changes camera distance. Keyboard shortcuts are optional.

Decks snap to a 3 m grid. Bulkheads, canopies and anchors require a deck. Leave a wall edge open as your doorway. A canopy restores charge while you stand under it.

Ion winds arrive every 150 seconds and last 45 seconds. At zero charge, health falls. At zero health, you return to the landing point with your equipment intact.

The broken ring on the ridge carries a faint repeating signal. A field cutter might let you read it.
