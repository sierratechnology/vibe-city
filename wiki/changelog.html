Update history

Controller support

Added standard gamepad input with a PS5 button layout, stick dead zones, menu navigation and saved button remapping in Field Guide → Settings. Controller inputs are cleared when the game loses focus or the device disconnects. Physical DualSense testing is still pending.

Field Guide cleanup

The Field Guide now has dedicated Fabrication, Backpack, Controls and Settings tabs. Open Field Guide → Settings to toggle tips. Navigation and Resume remain visible while content scrolls, including on phones.

Email verification clarity

Sending a verification email now opens a dedicated check-your-email screen. The original tab checks automatically for verification; email links show a confirmation with options to continue or close the tab.
