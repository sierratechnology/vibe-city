PLANETARY EXPEDITION · SEPTEMBER 2026

Update history

Camp gate

Builders can now place one freestanding low-tech Camp gate in any of four ground-edge rotations without a Deck. It shares authoritative edge occupancy, costs exactly three ferrite plus one fiber, and supports server-authoritative nearby operation with accessible Open/Close meaning. Closed gates block explorers and creatures; open gates allow traversal. Existing construction paths preserve persistence, ownership, dismantling and exact refunds. It adds no shelter, room sealing, pressure or life support, lock, PIN, role, permission, remote operation, automation, progression, structural support, weather protection, terrain editing or complete safety system.

Deck railing

Builders can now add a low, open Deck railing to any free supported Deck edge for one ferrite. It blocks explorer and creature crossing, rotates with the existing build controls, persists with the settlement and follows existing ownership and dismantling rules. It does not provide shelter, seal a room, support a Wall lumen, protect against weather or act as a lock or complete safety system.

Original broken signal ring asset

The Quiet Basin ruin now presents one original, locally stored low-poly broken-ring mesh in weathered ivory, dark blue-green metal, mint energy and restrained coral-lavender accents. Its existing reachable console, collision, scan progression, objective and lore remain unchanged. If the bounded asset is unavailable or invalid, the accepted primitive ruin remains available offline; this is one optimized visual tracer, not a complete art pass or asset platform.

Owner-managed Hydroponic bed access

A nearby Hydroponic bed builder can now grant or revoke planting and harvesting access for individual saved fellow explorers. The owner always retains access; collaborators spend their own water and fiber and receive their own harvest, while strangers remain unable to act. Access survives saves and reconnects, and revocation blocks the next action without changing an already planted bed or transferring ownership. This bounded ownership slice adds no crop choice, automation, groups, organizations, public roster or broader structure authority.

Truthful Hydroponic bed growth feedback

A planted nearby Hydroponic bed now shows a readable countdown based on accepted server world time and withholds Harvest until the accepted due is reached. The existing server-authoritative water + fiber planting cost, habitable-room requirement, 180-second duration and fiber + food harvest output are unchanged. This bounded interface slice adds no offline growth, automation, crop choice, ownership policy, complete farming system or physical-device verification.

Private Coral Shelf return guidance

After first contact, Terminal → Map now gives that same character accessible whole-meter distance and eight-direction compass bearing back to the immutable contact point. A subordinate clipped map line supports the text. Guidance follows the current accepted position and remains informational only: it adds no automated navigation, fast travel, quest, sharing, or public marker.

Private first Coral Shelf contact

Each character now privately retains the first server-accepted position where they physically entered Coral Shelf. Terminal → Map shows the waypoint and rounded coordinates after contact, including after reconnect or restart. The waypoint is informational, immutable, and never included in another character's or a public player summary.

Durable Coral Shelf discovery

Each authenticated character now privately records Quiet Basin through normal simulation and records Coral Shelf once after physically entering it. The bounded server-owned list survives saves and reconnects, while Terminal → Map exposes readable discovered-biome text and a bounded Coral Shelf cue. This does not add quests, achievements, rewards, analytics, or a complete biome.

Bounded Coral Shelf environment tracer

Coral Shelf now displays deterministic, nearby-loaded low-poly fan formations as its first bounded decorative environment tracer. Quiet Basin remains clear, while Coral Crown and canonical Coral Flux keep their separate landmark, resource, and interaction identities. This does not complete the biome or add an authored-asset pipeline, hazard, creature, resource, interaction, collision system, or full planet.

Seeded biome tracer

The protected starter region remains Quiet Basin, while seeded distant terrain can classify as Coral Shelf with a distinct deterministic palette and an accessible text label. This is visual classification only; complete regions, biome gameplay, hazards and physical-device verification remain future work.

Pending Workbench self-cancellation

A nearby explorer can now cancel only their own pending Field meal from the accessible Field Guide and receive exactly the reserved meat and fiber back once. Active and blocked-output jobs, another explorer's jobs, queue reordering, owner moderation, and bulk controls remain unavailable.

Shared Workbench permissions

A nearby Workbench owner can now grant or revoke meal-queue permission for individual saved collaborators from the accessible Field Guide. Authorized collaborators spend their own ingredients and receive their own exact-once output through the same one-active, two-pending queue; blocked output remains in place until its recipient has backpack space. Owner rosters, collaborator use meaning, revocation, and malformed saved grants are server-validated and viewer-specific. This bounded permission grants no ownership, dismantling, cargo, account, or administrative authority. Broader roles, groups, organizations, automation, recipes, queue reordering or moderation, and offline invitations remain planned.

Accessible local sound settings

Field Guide Settings now provides a persistent master mute and independent Interface and World/Gameplay volume controls with explicit values, keyboard, touch and controller-menu operation. Audio is supplementary, starts only after a user gesture, and uses bounded short generated cues for Field Guide actions and truthful server-result receipts. Preferences remain in this browser only; no account or world-save fields were added. There is no speech, music, downloaded media or remote audio provider. Automated desktop Chromium, phone-sized Chromium real-touch emulation and desktop Firefox are covered; physical phone speakers/headphones and physical controllers remain unverified.

Explicit cargo locker collaborator access

Builders of PIN-protected cargo lockers can grant or revoke one saved collaborator's full deposit and withdrawal access from the nearby Storage panel without sharing the PIN. The server validates ownership, proximity, exact request shape, saved-character identity and a 16-character cap before mutation. Revocation applies to the next transfer; changing the PIN clears keypad unlocks and explicit grants. Owner rosters and recipient access meaning are viewer-specific, version-one saves remain compatible, and malformed saved grants fail closed. Chromium desktop, phone-sized Chromium CDP touch, and Firefox desktop automation are verified; no physical phone or controller is claimed. No ownership, dismantling, construction, Workbench, account, admin, role, group, organization, offline invitation or automation authority is added.

Server-authoritative Workbench queue

Builders can place a freestanding Workbench through the authenticated construction flow, approach within 3 m, and queue one existing Field meal recipe through keyboard- or touch-accessible Field Guide controls. One active job and two pending jobs use five seconds of server-owned game time. Inputs reserve once after validation; completion grants once, persists across reconnect/save/reload, and blocks losslessly when output capacity changes. Queue details are owner-authorized, active work denies dismantling, and stopped servers do not provide offline progress. Chromium desktop, phone-sized Chromium CDP touch, and Firefox desktop automation are verified; no physical phone or controller is claimed.

Traversable Deck stairs

Builders can select a Deck stair through the authenticated Field Guide or mouse/touch hotbar, rotate it among four supported free deck edges, pay through server authority, and walk from terrain onto the current 0.27 m deck surface and back down. Placement rejects malformed, remote, unsupported, unaffordable, capped, edge-conflicting, resource-blocked and player-blocked requests before mutation. Rotation survives version-one saves, exact snapshots and reconnects. Phone-sized Chromium CDP touch and Chromium/Firefox desktop automation are verified; no physical phone or controller is claimed. Multi-level building, structural support, room enclosure, ladders, elevators and arbitrary vertical construction remain planned.

Operable sealed outer doors

Builders can select a sealed door in the authenticated construction flow and place it on a supported free deck edge with server-authoritative cost and collision checks. Nearby keyboard, touch and standard-controller interaction exposes the same Open sealed door or Close sealed door action. Closed doors remain visible, block passage and seal their edge; open doors remain visibly framed, permit traversal and make that edge unsealed. Their exact state persists through saves, snapshots and reconnects. This bounded addition does not provide locks, automatic doors, pressure equalization, airlock cycles, room pressurization, structural support, multi-level building, or generalized enclosure simulation.

Directional angled canopies

Builders can place an angled canopy above a deck, rotate its visible slope in four directions, and dismantle it with the existing ownership and collaborator safeguards. Flat and angled canopies share one roof slot and shelter the full tile beneath them. This is an architectural module, not water, weather-flow, structural-load, multi-level, airtight or enclosure simulation.

Passable doorway frames

Builders can now place a doorway frame on a free deck edge using keyboard, mouse or touch controls. The server validates support, range, cost, overlap and structure limits. The visible opening is traversable while its side posts remain solid; this is a static frame, not an operable sealed door.

Storage outage handling

Sign-in now explains database usage-limit failures instead of showing a generic world-storage error. Multiplayer persistence runs at four updates per second instead of ten, and failed writes back off to avoid a retry storm. A storage quota still requires restoration by the server operator.

Planet-generated day lengths

Each saved planet now has a seeded solar distance and a rotation period influenced by size and distance. Lighting, nocturnal creatures and sleep use that shared clock. Day/night lengths are no longer editable server settings. This is a gameplay generation rule; local time zones and seasons are future work.

Controller support

Added standard gamepad input with a PS5 button layout, stick dead zones, menu navigation and saved button remapping in Field Guide → Settings. Controller inputs are cleared when the game loses focus or the device disconnects. Physical DualSense testing is still pending.

Field Guide cleanup

The Field Guide now has dedicated Fabrication, Backpack, Controls and Settings tabs. Open Field Guide → Settings to toggle tips. Navigation and Resume remain visible while content scrolls, including on phones.

Email verification clarity

Sending a verification email now opens a dedicated check-your-email screen. The original tab checks automatically for verification; email links show a confirmation with options to continue or close the tab.
