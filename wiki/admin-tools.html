Admin Tools & roles

Open Field Guide (C or controller Options) → Admin Tools while signed in. This tab appears for the verified owner, admins and moderators. It shows only the tools permitted for your account.

Who can do what?

  • Owner: all admin tools, assign or remove staff roles, and moderate staff accounts.
  • Admin: quick build, spawn or drop resources and supplies, refill their own survival meters, change server settings, and ban or unban ordinary players.
  • Moderator: ban or unban ordinary players. No quick building, spawning, survival refills, server settings or staff assignment.
  • Player: ordinary gameplay with no Admin Tools access.

Roles belong to accounts. The owner is recognized through the verified owner email, never through a matching username. The server checks current permissions for every privileged request and records successful changes.

Assign an admin or moderator

  1. Have the player create an account and verify their email.
  2. As the owner, open Admin Tools and enter their account username, not their character name.
  3. Choose Admin or Moderator and apply the role. Choose Player to remove their staff privileges.
  4. The player should reopen Field Guide to refresh which staff tools are visible.

Only the owner can assign or revoke roles. Staff privileges do not automatically grant access to other players’ locked cargo or ownership of their structures.

Quick build

  1. Enable quick building in Admin Tools.
  2. Select a construction piece and use the piece selection button.
  3. Position the preview, rotate if needed, then use Action (E or R2) to place it.

Quick build bypasses material costs and the ruin unlock requirement. Grid, support, reach, collision and world-capacity checks still apply. Turn it off to return to normal construction costs. Moving existing structures, undo, flight and teleport are not available.

Spawn or drop supplies

Search for a resource or supply, choose an amount from 1 to 200, select your backpack or nearby ground as the destination, and spawn it. The backpack still holds 60 total item units, so leave enough room. Ground drops appear at your character’s feet. This tool creates the resources and supplies in its list; it does not directly spawn crafted weapons, tools or vehicles.

Refill and server settings

The refill action restores your own health, suit charge, oxygen, water, food and stamina. Server settings remain in Terminal → Server; see Servers & rules. World gravity is fixed at creation and cannot be changed through these settings.

Ban and unban

Enter the account username and choose Ban or Unban. Admins and moderators may moderate ordinary players; only the owner may moderate staff. Banned players are disconnected within 30 seconds. Unbanning allows the account to join again.

If the tab is missing

Refresh the game to load the latest release, then reopen Field Guide. Confirm that the owner assigned the role to the correct verified account username. A normal player account does not show the tab.
