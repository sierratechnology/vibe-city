PLANETARY EXPEDITION · SEPTEMBER 2026

Survive your own way.

Explore alone, work together, trade or fight. Vibe City is an original science-fiction survival sandbox.

Field Guide Settings includes browser-local master mute and separate Interface and World/Gameplay levels for short generated cues. Sound is optional and supplementary; there is no speech or music.

Nearby Workbench owners can grant individual saved collaborators permission to queue Field meals using their own ingredients and receive their own output. Each explorer may cancel only their own pending Field meal for an exact ingredient refund; active and blocked-output jobs cannot be cancelled. The bounded grant does not include ownership, dismantling, cargo, account, or administrative authority.

Latest release: one Action control, a separate Jump button, saved planetary gravity, gradual sun-driven lighting, and owner-managed admins and moderators. Read the update history. Refresh the game to load the new controls.
