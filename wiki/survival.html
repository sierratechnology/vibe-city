PLANETARY EXPEDITION · SEPTEMBER 2026

Survival & equipment

Keep separate reserves of food, hydration, oxygen and suit charge. Stamina supports exertion and recovers when you rest. Sprinting drains stamina; low food slows its recovery. Unsafe air and cold are dangerous without a suit.

Water

Gather ice and build an ice processor. Transfer ice into its tank through Terminal → Habitat; melting uses suit power. Fill portable water canisters, then drink them or supply a hydroponic bed. Automated pipes are not implemented yet.

Food

Hunt Mossbacks for meat and craft field meals with ribbon fiber. Hydroponic beds grow fiber and meals inside a habitable room after receiving water and fiber.

Equipment and belt

The Field Guide crafts the cutter, flashlight, repair tool and arc carbine. The carbine consumes flux crystals per shot. A ten-slot belt provides shortcuts to carried items and equipped tools; it does not add inventory capacity. Assign slots in Terminal → Equipment.

Skills

Mining, construction, combat and piloting gain experience through use. Mining experience modestly improves gathering speed, combat experience improves damage, and piloting experience improves rover efficiency. Construction experience is recorded for future building specializations.
