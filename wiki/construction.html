PLANETARY EXPEDITION · SEPTEMBER 2026

Bases & airlocks

Quick storage and home bunks

Storage now has Store matching, Store all and Take all; Shift-click transfers a whole stack up to available space. Each shortcut honors PINs, collaborator grants and capacity. Set your own nearby Bunk as home in Habitat to recover beside it at zero health, with landing as fallback if it is gone or blocked. Backpack, storage and home instructions.

Space construction tools

The Fabrication driver is your starter construction multitool. It is added to an empty belt slot without replacing existing assignments. Use its belt slot or B to build, R to rotate a preview, and V to reclaim. In reclaim mode Action dismantles the nearest authorized piece once, with the existing support, ownership, collaborator and refund checks. Controller users can choose Build, Reclaim or Stow under Field Guide → Fabrication. Field cutters handle mining and salvage; the repair tool is now named Arc welder and retains its existing repair costs. Editing here means preview rotation and reclaim/rebuild, not moving finished structures.

Furniture placement and quick controls

Lockers, workbenches, beds and other floor equipment now move in 0.25 m steps. Aim the preview toward an edge to arrange multiple objects on one deck; each object must fit without overlapping another object or wall. Floors and walls retain their 3 m grid. The suit flashlight follows the character’s facing direction. Open Backpack directly with keyboard I or controller Create / View; press again to close. The right-side controls are reduced to Action and Jump; secondary controls are in Field Guide → Controls. Action enters a nearby vehicle and exits while aboard.

Building controls and quick build

Select a piece in Field Guide → Fabrication, then use the labeled Action button, E or controller R2 to place it. Click or tap terrain also places the preview in build mode. R or controller L2 rotates it. Admins and the owner can enable material-free quick building in Field Guide → Admin Tools; placement still requires valid support, space and range.

Cargo collaborator access

A cargo locker builder can set a PIN, then grant or revoke one saved collaborator's full deposit and withdrawal access from the nearby Storage panel without disclosing that PIN. Revocation applies to the collaborator's next transfer, and changing the PIN clears both prior keypad unlocks and explicit collaborator grants. The grant does not confer ownership, dismantling, construction, Workbench, account or administrative authority. Storage roles, groups, organizations, offline invitations and automation are not included.

Workbench queue

The freestanding Workbench costs 6 ferrite and 2 ribbon fiber. Its builder can approach within 3 m and use Field Guide → Fabrication to queue a Field meal for 1 meat and 1 fiber. The server reserves inputs once, runs one five-second job with two pending slots, and delivers each meal once. Full output remains blocked without losing value. Workbench progress requires the server to be running; there is no offline catch-up. Finish all work before dismantling.

Construction snaps to a three-metre grid. Green previews indicate valid, affordable placement. Field Guide → Fabrication lists every available piece. Keep a doorway accessible.

Deck stairs

A Deck stair snaps to one free edge of a real deck and rotates among all four edges. Select it in the Field Guide or with the mouse/touch hotbar; it has no digit shortcut. Its visible ramp and walking surface provide a low-rise transition between surrounding terrain and the current 0.27 m deck top. The server checks support, grid identity, range, cost, structure limits, edge conflicts, resources and players before placement. This does not create a second story, structural support, room enclosure, ladders, elevators, or arbitrary vertical building; those systems remain planned.

Doorway frames

A doorway frame snaps to any free edge of a deck. Its opening is always passable; the two side posts remain solid. It has no open or closed state and is not an airtight door. Select it in the Field Guide, with hotbar slot 8, or with the touch build controls.

Angled canopies

An angled canopy snaps above a supporting deck and shares that tile's single roof slot with the flat canopy. Rotate it to change the visible slope direction. Like the flat canopy, it shelters the full tile beneath it. It does not simulate drainage, weather flow, structural loads, multiple levels, airtightness or room enclosure.

Sealed outer doors

A sealed door snaps to a supported free deck edge and remains visibly framed whether open or closed. Approach it and use the contextual interaction: Open sealed door makes the edge traversable and unsealed; Close sealed door blocks passage and seals that edge. Placement, payment, operation and saved state are server-authoritative and survive reconnects. This basic door adds no locks, automatic trigger, pressure equalization, airlock cycle, room pressurization, structural-support rule, multi-level building, or generalized enclosure simulation.

Airlock layout

Outside → sealed outer door → small enclosed chamber → airlock door → enclosed base with life support.

Every room needs floors, ceilings and sealed wall edges. A door placed directly between a base and the outdoors is not a valid airlock. A chamber can contain up to nine floor tiles. Close both doors, power the base life-support unit, then cycle the airlock from Terminal → Habitat. Once both sides are habitable, the inner door opens.

Life support consumes its reserve according to room size. Refuel it with flux crystals. Opening a room to the outdoors or damaging its enclosure makes it unsafe. Seal the breach and restore pressure before removing your suit.

Sleep

Approach a bunk inside a habitable shelter and remove your suit. By default, all connected players must sleep to skip the night. Recent combat prevents skipping. Disconnected players do not count.

Storage

Lockers show your backpack and locker as two item grids with icons and stack counts. Activate a backpack slot to store, or a locker slot to take. Choose a quantity or ALL; transfers are limited to available items and destination space. D-pad or arrow keys navigate, Cross activates, and L1/R1 switches inventory. Your selected slot and controller focus stay in place after a transfer, including when a stack becomes empty. Collaborator controls are under Locker access & collaborators.

Backpacks hold 60 item units. Cargo lockers hold 200. Builders can set a four-to-eight-digit PIN using the Storage keypad. Other characters must enter the code; changing it revokes their remembered access. Repeated wrong guesses temporarily lock the keypad. PIN locks do not prevent destructive raids.
