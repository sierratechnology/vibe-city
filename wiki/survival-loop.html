Gather, build, store.

Your first expedition has a simple rhythm: collect materials, make a cutter, create shelter, and keep spare supplies in a locker. Open Field Guide → First expedition for an optional checklist. It stays inside the guide and remembers completed steps with your character.

1. Gather and craft

Approach ferrite or ribbon fiber and hold Action (E or controller R2). Gather 3 ferrite and 2 fiber, then open Fabrication and craft a Field cutter. The cutter improves yield. Search the recipe catalog by name or ingredient, or filter Tools, Building, Equipment and Food. Every recipe shows what you have and what you need. TRACK pins one material list inside Fabrication; this pin stays in this browser for this character.

2. Make a foothold

Your starter Fabrication driver is in an empty belt slot. B or its belt slot equips build mode. Select a Deck, find a green preview, then use Action. Rotate previews with R or controller L2. A deck costs 2 ferrite and 1 fiber; a Canopy costs 1 ferrite and 3 fiber. Add the canopy over your deck for shelter. Lockers and furniture can be arranged in quarter-metre steps. V enters reclaim mode; ownership, support and refund-space rules still apply.

3. Use your backpack

Press I or controller Create / View. Each resource and tool has a permanent slot, icon and count. Select an item to inspect it, eat or drink supplies, toggle a flashlight, assign a belt slot, or drop a chosen resource quantity. Tools do not count toward the 60-item capacity and cannot be dropped. Dropped resources are public: anyone nearby can gather them. They remain in the world save until collected. Use Field Guide → Backpack with a mouse or touch screen.

4. Store quickly

A Cargo locker costs 6 ferrite and 2 fiber. Approach within 3 m and use Action. Store matching fills only material types already in the locker; Store all moves what fits; Take all fills available backpack space. Lockers hold 200 item units. These shortcuts use the same PIN and collaborator access rules as individual transfers.

Click a slot for the selected quantity, or Shift-click to move the whole available stack up to capacity. D-pad/arrows move between stable slots; Cross transfers, L1/R1 switches sides, and Circle/Escape closes. Selecting a depleted stack keeps your focus in place.

5. Claim home

Build a Bunk on a deck for 2 ferrite and 4 fiber. Approach your own bunk within 3 m, use Action, and choose Set home bunk in Habitat. At zero health you recover beside it with your equipment intact. If the bunk was destroyed, removed, or has no clear recovery position, you return to landing. Claiming a bunk does not make the room habitable; sleeping still requires the usual safe-room and suit rules. Clear the home selection from that bunk's Habitat controls.

Feedback and controls

Mining strikes, steady fabrication/welding poses, landing compression and small tool sparks help show actions. Footsteps, landing, gathering, construction, crafting and storage have distinct original generated sounds. Field Guide → Settings controls mute and separate interface/gameplay volume. Browser reduced-motion preferences suppress tool sparks and panel entrance animation. No extra Backpack button or center-screen instruction panel has been added.
