Privacy notice

Version 2026-09-16. Vibe City processes the data needed to provide accounts and persistent multiplayer worlds.

What is stored

Account usernames, email addresses, salted password hashes, verification status, session records, consent versions and marketing preferences are stored alongside character and game progress. Raw passwords are not stored. Security rate limits use hashed network identifiers. Hosting providers may retain operational request logs.

Purposes and providers

We use this information to authenticate players, deliver requested account emails, save progress, enforce server permissions, investigate abuse and operate the game. Hosting is provided through Vercel; persistent cloud data uses Upstash Redis; verification email delivery uses Resend when configured. Providers receive the information required for their role.

Cookies and preferences

An essential HttpOnly session cookie keeps you signed in. Browser storage remembers your selected character and display preferences such as tips. Newsletter and marketing emails require the optional signup preference, which can be changed from your account controls. No marketing campaign is sent by the prototype itself.

Retention and requests

Account and game data are retained while needed to operate your account and world. Verification links expire after 30 minutes; sessions expire after seven days; daily world snapshots expire after eight days. To request access, correction or deletion, ask the operator for a private contact route through project support. Do not publish private account information there.
