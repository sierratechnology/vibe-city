PLANETARY EXPEDITION · SEPTEMBER 2026

Planet & exploration

The first spherical planet measures 145 km around the equator. Travelling continuously around it returns to your starting location. Nearby terrain is streamed as you move, including across the poles and longitude seam.

Use Terminal → Map to view surveyed regions, discovered monuments and personal markers. The basic scout travels at approximately 24 km/h on level ground before cargo penalties, giving a reference equatorial journey of about six hours without stops.

Atmosphere

Atmospheric conditions are seeded. Use Terminal → Equipment → Test atmosphere to read oxygen, pressure, contamination and temperature. Unknown air is not assumed safe. Your suit analyzer uses battery power.

Monuments

  • Broken Relay: the first technology discovery and signal archive.
  • Cryowell Station: an abandoned ice-processing landmark with recoverable supplies.
  • Crawler Graveyard: industrial wreckage and vehicle materials.

The first three landmarks have authored starting-region locations. Terrain and resource/salvage deposits are seeded. More elaborate randomized monument interiors and additional distant monument variants are future work.

Later

Additional planets, spacecraft, orbital stations and interstellar travel remain planned.
