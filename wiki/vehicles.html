Construct a rover from Terminal → Vehicles, or find a seeded wreck and repair it with a repair tool, ferrite and conductive ore. The first occupant drives with the normal movement controls; others ride as passengers. Cargo weight reduces speed. Recharge with flux crystals.

Expansion modules add battery capacity, 100 cargo units, or oxygen support for occupants. Each costs four ferrite and two conductive ore. The crawler reserves a rear platform for future equipment attachments. Drills, cranes and other attachments are not yet functional.
