VISUAL UPDATE · SEPTEMBER 24, 2026

Graphics & animation

Tool and landing feedback

Fabrication and welding now use a steadier arm pose than mining strikes, and the explorer briefly compresses on landing. Nearby explorer names are limited to eight non-overlapping labels so a busy settlement stays readable. Tool sparks use a bounded particle pool and respect reduced-motion preferences. Original synthesized cues distinguish movement, gathering, building, crafting and storage. Audio remains optional, with independent volume controls.

Space construction tools

The Fabrication driver is your starter construction multitool. It is added to an empty belt slot without replacing existing assignments. Use its belt slot or B to build, R to rotate a preview, and V to reclaim. In reclaim mode Action dismantles the nearest authorized piece once, with the existing support, ownership, collaborator and refund checks. Controller users can choose Build, Reclaim or Stow under Field Guide → Fabrication. Field cutters handle mining and salvage; the repair tool is now named Arc welder and retains its existing repair costs. Editing here means preview rotation and reclaim/rebuild, not moving finished structures.

Some metal, salvage and flux deposits are embedded in crashed meteor fragments. Ice deposits appear as glacial spires or frozen basins. Use Action near the deposit to mine its existing resources. These formations preserve saved deposit positions and quantities; they are not new walkable mountains or lake terrain.

Ground cover now uses irregular, persistent scatter positions with varied sizes and density. Vehicle movement and jump height interpolate between server snapshots for smoother presentation.

Vibe City uses an original stylized science-fiction direction: ivory expedition equipment, dark teal frames, coral accents and mint light. The new locally authored model set replaces the basic explorer, wildlife, vehicle, resource and equipment shapes.

Models in this release

  • An articulated explorer with a pressure suit, visor, expedition backpack, field cutter and arc carbine.
  • Distinct Mossback, Bristletick and Veilstalker models.
  • Scout, Expedition rover and Transport crawler models with rolling wheels.
  • Eleven resource and supply models, from mineral clusters and ribbon leaves to water canisters and field meals.
  • Cargo locker, Workbench, life support, ice processor, hydroponic bed, bunk and resonance anchor equipment.

Motion

The explorer has idle motion, walking and running leg/arm movement, a jumping pose and local tool-use gestures. Creatures have moving legs and the Veilstalker has a swaying tail. Vehicles animate their wheels as they travel. Movement and jumping follow the server’s accepted world state. Tool gestures are visual feedback; the server still decides whether an action succeeds.

Terrain and sky

Close terrain now follows the world’s surface in more detail. Sand grain, mineral color variation, small grasses and pebbles give the ground texture. Small decorative plants and stones are scenery, not harvestable resources. The sky adds a horizon gradient, soft clouds and stars, driven by the existing sun-based lighting. Construction edges are softened while placement and collision rules remain unchanged.

Loading and compatibility

The model pack loads with the game. If it cannot load, the existing shapes keep the world playable. Refresh after the release to load the new visuals. Your saved characters, world, items and buildings carry over. Further art refinements remain possible; this release does not add new species, planets or gameplay systems.
