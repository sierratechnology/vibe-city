Getting started

A guided first expedition

Open Field Guide → First expedition for a saved, optional gathering, crafting, shelter, storage and home checklist. Follow the full gather–build–store guide. Backpack opens with I or controller Create / View. Fabrication now includes categories, search, have/need counts and a tracked recipe.

Before entering

Create an account with a username, email and password. Accept the terms and verify your email. Marketing emails are optional. Existing accounts can add an email without losing their characters. Each account supports three selectable characters.

Your first foothold

  1. Gather three ferrite and two ribbon fiber near landing.
  2. Open Field Guide and craft a field cutter.
  3. Build a deck and canopy. Seek the Broken Relay to unlock a resonance anchor.
  4. Explore for ice, conductive ore, silica, carbon and salvage.
  5. Construct life support inside a fully enclosed base before removing your suit.

Controls

WASD moves; Shift sprints; drag the world to orbit the camera; click or tap terrain to walk. E uses the labeled Action button to gather, interact, attack or place a build. Space jumps. F toggles the flashlight, H eats, C opens Field Guide, M opens the expedition terminal, and B toggles building. In build mode, 1–7 select the original construction pieces; other pieces are in Fabrication. Outside build mode, 1–0 activate belt slots.

Mouse and touch actions are available through on-screen buttons. The movement pad appears only on touch-capable devices. Field Guide → Settings → Show tips hides tutorial hints and objectives while retaining warnings and action results.

Settings

Open Field Guide (C) → Settings to turn tips and objectives on or off. Preferences are saved automatically in your browser. The guide also has separate Fabrication, Backpack and Controls tabs. Authorized staff see an Admin Tools tab.

PS5 controller

Connect your controller over Bluetooth or USB, then press a button with the game focused. Left stick moves; right stick looks. Options opens the Field Guide; Circle goes back. Triangle toggles building, R2 uses the labeled Action button, R3 jumps, L2 rotates, L1/R1 select a belt slot or building piece, Cross uses the belt slot, and holding L3 sprints. D-pad: up flashlight, down food, left locker, right map. In menus, D-pad navigates and Cross selects; left/right changes a focused selection list. Customize gameplay buttons in Field Guide → Settings. Text entry requires a keyboard. Requires a browser exposing a standard Gamepad API mapping; physical DualSense testing is pending.

Planetary clock

The planet’s generated size and solar distance influence its rotation period. The shared server clock divides that period equally for wildlife and sleeping rules; visible daylight depends on your location on the planet. Find solar distance and rotation in Terminal → Server. This gameplay rule is not an astronomy simulation: rotation controls a real planet’s day; distance primarily affects its year and received sunlight. Lighting now follows the sun’s local elevation; wildlife and sleep use the shared server clock. Axial tilt and seasons are not implemented.

One action button and Jump

The main action button changes its label to match the nearby target: gather, open or close a door, access cargo, use a workbench, interact with habitat equipment, attack an aimed creature or player, or place a building. Keyboard E and controller R2 use the same action. Read the label before pressing: the nearest eligible target determines the action, and attacks require aiming toward the target. Holding repeats gathering or attacks only for the original target; release and press again to switch targets. Jump uses its own button, Space, or controller R3. Jump costs stamina, and the server applies the planet’s saved gravity. Walls remain blocking; this release does not add vaulting.
