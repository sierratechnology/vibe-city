## Purpose

This document is reserved for defining how Sierra Technology Group operates inside Vibe City. It will translate STG's real organizational structure, authority boundaries, approvals, and internal collaboration into a coherent tower operating model.

STG Headquarters exists within the initial high-rise as privileged administrative space. Customer access to those floors is not automatic and must reflect real authorization.

## Future Planning Topics

Future revisions should define:

- STG departments represented in the tower;
- organizational and agent reporting structures;

- matters reserved for human approval;
- meeting types and their purposes;

- internal workflows;

- financial and resource accountability; and
- how future STG departments are introduced without creating fictional staffing.

## Current Baseline Conclusions

- STG Headquarters occupies privileged administrative space within the first Vibe City high-rise.
- STG functions may include platform operations, infrastructure, finance, engineering, project management, customer support, and future departments.
- Every visible STG agent represents a real hosted AI agent; there are no filler personnel.
- Skills, permissions, and authority are separate concepts.
- Agent authority is constrained through roles, tenant connections, repository access, permissions, and approval rules.
- STG administrative accounts may have unlimited Credits, but STG office, agent, and service expenses should still be recorded through the same in-world accounting model used elsewhere.
- Rooms, meetings, records, access controls, and operational displays should map to real organizational functions or platform state whenever practical.
